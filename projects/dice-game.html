How to Play

Rules

1

Roll the dice to add its value to your current round score.

2

If you roll a 1, your current round points are lost and your turn ends.

3

Press Hold to save your round points into your total score.

4

Reach the target score before the computer to win.

Concept

Game Logic

Uses conditional logic, random values and game-state management to control turns, scoring and winning conditions.

Java Focus

Random Numbers

The original Java concept uses random number generation to simulate dice rolls between one and six.

Control Flow
